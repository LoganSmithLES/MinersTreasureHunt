Overview:
>Your goal as a miner is to exit the mines in a better status than your fellow miners!
>Leaving with more time, money & ores puts you higher on the leaderboard!
>If you run out of time while mining, you will suffocate!
>If you run out of money and can't pay the exit toll, you suffocate and die!

Controls:
> Use WASD to move up (W), down (s), left (a), and right (d)
> Use i to open up the store

Characters, Ores & Store:
> The store is where you can sell your ores and buy mining equipment.
> While in the store your timer stops!
> You can only carry 10 of each ore! 
> You can only sell all of one ore OR all of your ores at once.
> The following is each map character:
   # = Border Wall (Can't walk/interact with)
   P = Player (Thats you!)
   X = Bolder (Impassable, But Removable!)
   G = Gold! ($5)
   C = Cobalt! ($4)
   I = Iron! ($3)
   N = Nickle ($2)
   E = Exit! ($100 to exit!)
